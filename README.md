## 📖 Overview

**FLAG.UZ** is an educational web project designed to help students explore the history, symbolism, and significance of the national flag of Uzbekistan.

Rather than presenting information through conventional static articles, the platform combines historical explanations, visual storytelling, interactive elements, and knowledge assessments to create a more engaging learning experience.

The project aims to make information about Uzbekistan's national flag more accessible, understandable, and memorable, particularly for younger generations.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Historical Exploration
- An organised historical overview of the national flag.
- Important milestones surrounding its adoption.
- Contextual explanations of its historical significance.

### 2. Symbolism and Meaning
- Exploration of the flag's colours and visual elements.
- Explanations of the crescent and twelve stars.
- A structured presentation of national symbolism.

### 3. Interactive Knowledge Assessment
- A 10-question knowledge assessment.
- Questions designed to reinforce historical understanding.
- An opportunity for users to evaluate their knowledge.

### 4. Civic Awareness
- Educational content about the importance of national symbols.
- Emphasis on civic responsibility and national identity.
- A dedicated section connecting younger generations with the country's future.

### 5. Educational Resources
- Historical and informational content.
- References to relevant official legislation.
- An accessible structure intended for secondary-school students.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

FLAG.UZ was developed around several educational objectives:

- Make historical information easier to explore.
- Encourage independent learning through interactive content.
- Improve awareness of national symbols and their significance.
- Connect historical knowledge with civic education.
- Demonstrate how digital platforms can support modern educational experiences.

## 👥 Intended Audience

The platform is primarily designed for:

- Secondary-school students.
- Teachers and educators.
- Young people interested in Uzbekistan's history.
- Anyone seeking to understand the country's national symbols.
